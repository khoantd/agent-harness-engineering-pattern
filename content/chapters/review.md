## Summary

After the book was written, every entry was checked again by a separate reviewer acting as a senior architect for business AI platforms. Each entry was compared with three things: the pattern's original definition in the standard books, how real agent systems actually behave, and what large companies need (keeping customers' data apart, controlling who can do what, keeping records for audits, protecting personal data, controlling cost, and seeing what the system is doing). Mistakes were fixed in place, and every entry gained an "In a large company" note. This appendix lists what was found and what changed.
