How faithful is each fit?

Each pattern has a badge beside its name. Direct fit means the agent system uses the pattern exactly as the books describe it. Adapted fit means it is the real pattern with a small twist for AI agents. Loose fit means the agent system borrows the idea more than the exact structure. Counts: 157 direct, 35 adapted, 0 loose. The loose fits are listed here.
